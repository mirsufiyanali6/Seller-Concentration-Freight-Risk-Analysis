# KPI Definitions

Reference for every metric shown across the five dashboards in `Seller_Concentration___Freight_Risk_Analysis.pbix`. Formulas are written against the source tables in `Business_Solution.sql` / `Data_Directory.md`.

## Marketplace Executive Overview

| KPI | Definition | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| Total Revenue | Sum of all item-level prices (excludes freight). | `SUM(order_items.price)` |
| Total Orders | Count of distinct orders. | `COUNT(DISTINCT orders.order_id)` |
| Total Customers | Count of distinct people, using the person-level key (not the order-scoped `customer_id`). | `COUNT(DISTINCT customers.customer_unique_id)` |
| Total Sellers | Count of distinct active sellers. | `COUNT(DISTINCT sellers.seller_id)` |
| Average Order Value | Total revenue divided by total orders. | `SUM(price) / COUNT(DISTINCT order_id)` |
| Monthly Revenue Trend | Revenue summed by calendar month of purchase. | `SUM(price) GROUP BY MONTH(order_purchase_timestamp)` |
| Order Status Distribution | Share of orders in each status (delivered, shipped, canceled, unavailable). | `COUNT(order_id) GROUP BY order_status` |

## Seller Concentration & Dependency

| KPI | Definition | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| Top Seller Revenue | Revenue generated by the single highest-earning seller. | `MAX(seller-level SUM(price))` |
| Top 10 Seller Revenue | Combined revenue of the ten highest-earning sellers. | `SUM(price)` for the top 10 sellers by revenue |
| Top 10 Seller Revenue % | Top 10 seller revenue as a share of total marketplace revenue. | `Top 10 Seller Revenue / Total Revenue` |
| Average Revenue per Seller | Total revenue divided by the count of active sellers — the benchmark the top sellers are compared against. | `SUM(price) / COUNT(DISTINCT seller_id)` |
| Seller Revenue by State | Revenue attributed to the seller's registered state. | `SUM(price) GROUP BY seller_state` |
| Seller Revenue vs Freight Burden | Per-seller total revenue plotted against per-seller total freight cost, to spot high-revenue sellers who are also high-freight-cost outliers. | Seller-level `SUM(price)` vs `SUM(freight_value)` |

## Freight & Logistics Risk

| KPI | Definition | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| Total Freight | Sum of all freight charges across order items. | `SUM(order_items.freight_value)` |
| Average Freight | Mean freight cost per order item. | `AVG(freight_value)` |
| High Freight % | Share of order items where freight cost alone exceeds a defined high-cost threshold. | `COUNT(freight_value > threshold) / COUNT(*)` |
| Freight to Price % | Total freight cost as a share of total item price — the aggregate cost-efficiency ratio of shipping relative to goods value. | `SUM(freight_value) / SUM(price)` |
| Average Freight: Same State vs Cross State | Mean freight value split by whether the seller and customer are in the same state. | `AVG(freight_value) GROUP BY (seller_state = customer_state)` |
| Freight Cost by Seller State | Total freight cost attributed to the seller's state. | `SUM(freight_value) GROUP BY seller_state` |
| Order Distribution by Freight Band | Count of orders bucketed into freight-cost bands (0–10, 10–20, 20–30, 30–50, 50+). | `COUNT(order_id) GROUP BY freight_band` |

## Payment & Installment Risk

| KPI | Definition | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| Total Payment Value | Sum of all recorded payments. | `SUM(order_payments.payment_value)` |
| Average Payment Value | Mean payment value per payment record. | `AVG(payment_value)` |
| Average Installments | Mean number of installments used per payment. | `AVG(payment_installments)` |
| High Installment Payment % | Share of payments split across a high number of installments (7+), representing deferred-revenue exposure. | `COUNT(payment_installments >= 7) / COUNT(*)` |
| Payment Mix by Installment Group | Total payment value bucketed into installment-count groups (1, 2–3, 4–6, 7+). | `SUM(payment_value) GROUP BY installment_group` |
| Payment Value by Payment Type | Total payment value split by method (credit card, boleto, voucher, debit card). | `SUM(payment_value) GROUP BY payment_type` |

## Cross-Sell & Growth

| KPI | Definition | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| Multi Category Orders | Count of orders containing products from more than one category. | `COUNT(order_id) WHERE category_count > 1` |
| Multi Category Order % | Multi-category orders as a share of all orders. | `Multi Category Orders / Total Orders` |
| Average Categories per Order | Mean number of distinct categories per order. | `AVG(category_count)` |
| Revenue Growth % | Percentage change in revenue versus the prior comparable period. | `(Current Period Revenue − Prior Period Revenue) / Prior Period Revenue` |
| Category Revenue Contribution | Revenue split between single-category and multi-category orders. | `SUM(price) GROUP BY (category_count = 1)` |
| Top Categories by Revenue | Categories ranked by total revenue. | `SUM(price) GROUP BY product_category_name_english` |
